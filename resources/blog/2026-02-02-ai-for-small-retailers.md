## The Daily Struggles of Small Retailers

If you run a small retail business, these scenarios probably sound familiar:

**Customer on the phone**: "Do you have that blue dress in size medium?"
*You scramble through inventory, clicking through screens, trying to find the answer quickly.*

**End of month**: "What were our best-selling products? How much did we make from that promotion?"
*You spend hours pulling together data from different sources to create a report.*

**New supplier email**: "What products do we need to reorder?"
*You check stock levels one by one, hoping you don't miss anything.*

These tasks aren't hard - they're just time-consuming. And that's exactly the kind of work AI excels at.

## Real-World Use Cases

Let's look at how Scribe can help with actual daily tasks:

### Inventory Search

Instead of clicking through menus and filters, simply ask:

> "Show me all products with less than 10 items in stock"

Scribe instantly returns a list of low-stock items. Need more specific information?

> "Which products in the Electronics category are running low?"

You get exactly what you need, when you need it.

### Business Insights

At the end of the day, week, or month, understanding your business performance is crucial. Ask Scribe:

> "What were my top 5 best-selling products this month?"

Or dig deeper:

> "Compare this month's sales to last month"

Scribe analyzes your order data and provides clear, actionable insights without you having to create pivot tables or complex reports.

### Customer Information

When a customer calls, you need information fast:

> "Show me all orders from John Smith"

> "What's the contact info for Riverside Suppliers?"

Scribe retrieves customer and contact information instantly, so you can provide excellent service without making people wait.

### Product Management

Adding new products used to mean filling out multiple form fields. With Scribe:

> "Create a new product called Vintage Leather Bag, price $89, in the Accessories category"

Scribe handles the creation, and you can review and adjust the details as needed.

### Quick Answers

Sometimes you just need a quick number:

> "How many orders did we complete today?"

> "What's the total value of pending invoices?"

> "How many products do we have in the Clothing category?"

Instant answers without navigating through dashboards.
