Running a small business shouldn't require a degree in enterprise software. Yet for too long, that's exactly what it has felt like. Multiple apps, complex dashboards, expensive subscriptions, and steep learning curves have made business management unnecessarily difficult for the people who need simplicity the most.

That's why we built Folio.

## The Meaning Behind "Folio"

The name **Folio** comes from the concept of a *portfolio* or *collection*. In historical contexts, a folio was a large sheet of paper folded once to create pages for important documents, often bound together to preserve valuable records.

For us, Folio represents **all your business data in one place** - a single, organized collection of everything that matters to your business:

- Your products and inventory
- Your customers and contacts
- Your orders and transactions
- Your invoices and payments
- Your insights and analytics

No more jumping between five different apps. No more copy-pasting data from spreadsheets. No more wondering where that customer's information ended up. Folio brings it all together.

## Meet Scribe: Your AI Business Assistant

Every great business historically had a **Scribe** - a trusted clerk who meticulously recorded transactions, managed documents, and kept the business running smoothly. The scribe was the person you could rely on to know where everything was and to help you make sense of the numbers.

That's exactly what our AI assistant, **Scribe**, is designed to be.

Scribe isn't just another chatbot. It's a knowledgeable assistant that understands your business data and can:

- **Search your inventory** - "Show me all products under $50"
- **Provide insights** - "What were my best-selling items last month?"
- **Create records** - "Add a new product called Summer Dress, priced at $45"
- **Answer questions** - "Who are my top 5 customers by order value?"
- **Generate reports** - "Give me a summary of this week's orders"

Think of Scribe as your digital business partner, always available to help you understand and manage your business better.

## The Problems We're Solving

Small business owners face three major challenges when it comes to business management software:

### Complexity

Enterprise tools are built for large companies with dedicated IT teams. They're packed with features most small businesses will never use, making them overwhelming and difficult to navigate. We believe software should work for you, not the other way around.

### Cost

Many business management solutions charge hundreds of dollars per month, putting them out of reach for solo entrepreneurs and small retailers. Premium features often cost extra, and before you know it, your software costs more than your monthly rent.

### Time

Learning new software takes time. Switching between multiple apps takes time. Manually entering data takes time. For small business owners who are already wearing multiple hats, time is the most precious resource.

Folio addresses all three by providing an intuitive, affordable, all-in-one platform that just works.
