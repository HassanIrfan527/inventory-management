# Quick Start Guide

This guide will help you get started with Folio in under 10 minutes.

## Step 1: Set Up Your Company

Before you start adding products and contacts, let's configure your company information.

1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Company Info**
2. Upload your company logo
3. Fill in your company name, email, phone, and website
4. Click **Save**

Your company details will appear on all invoices you generate.

## Step 2: Create Product Categories

Organizing your products into categories makes them easier to find and manage.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Product Categories**
2. Enter a category name (e.g., "Electronics", "Clothing", "Services")
3. Click **Add Category**
4. Repeat for all your product categories

## Step 3: Add Your First Product

Now let's add a product to your inventory.

1. Navigate to **Inventory** from the sidebar
2. Click the **Add Product** button
3. Fill in the product details:
   - **Name**: Your product name
   - **SKU**: A unique identifier (auto-generated if left blank)
   - **Purchase Price**: What you paid for it
   - **Retail Price**: What you'll sell it for
   - **Stock Quantity**: How many you have
4. Select a category
5. Upload product images (optional)
6. Click **Save**

## Step 4: Add a Contact

Add your first customer or supplier.

1. Go to **Contacts** from the sidebar
2. Click **Add Contact**
3. Choose the contact type (Customer, Supplier, or Lead)
4. Enter their details
5. Click **Save**

## Step 5: Create Your First Order

Ready to make a sale? Let's create an order.

1. Navigate to **Orders**
2. Click **Create Order**
3. Select or create a customer
4. Add products to the order
5. Review and confirm
6. The order is created and inventory is updated automatically
