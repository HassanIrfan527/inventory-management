# Managing Products

Products are at the heart of your inventory. This guide covers everything you need to know about managing your product catalog.

## Product Overview

Each product in Folio can have the following information:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Name | The product name displayed everywhere |
| SKU | Stock Keeping Unit - a unique identifier |
| Purchase Price | Your cost to acquire the product |
| Retail Price | The price you sell it for |
| Stock Quantity | Current inventory level |
| Categories | One or more categories for organization |
| Images | Multiple product images |
| Status | Active, Draft, or Archived |
| Notes | Internal notes (not shown to customers) |

## Adding a New Product

1. Click **Inventory** in the sidebar
2. Click the **Add Product** button
3. Fill in the required fields (Name, at minimum)
4. Set your pricing:
   - **Purchase Price**: Your cost
   - **Retail Price**: Selling price
   - **Delivery Charges**: Optional shipping cost
5. Enter the current **Stock Quantity**
6. Select one or more **Categories**
7. Upload product **Images** (drag and drop supported)
8. Add any internal **Notes**
9. Click **Save**

## Editing Products

To edit an existing product:

1. Go to **Inventory**
2. Find the product (use search or filters)
3. Click on the product card
4. Make your changes
5. Click **Save**

## Bulk Category Assignment

Need to assign categories to multiple products at once?

1. Go to **Inventory**
2. Select multiple products using the checkboxes
3. Click **Bulk Actions** > **Assign Categories**
4. Choose the categories
5. Click **Apply**

## Low Stock Alerts

Folio can notify you when products are running low:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Notifications**
2. Enable **Low Stock Alerts**
3. Set your threshold (e.g., alert when stock falls below 10)

## Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar to find products by:
- Product name
- SKU
- Description

Use filters to narrow down by:
- Category
- Stock status (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock)
- Price range

## Best Practices

1. **Use descriptive names** - Make product names clear and searchable
2. **Always set SKUs** - Unique identifiers help prevent confusion
3. **Keep stock updated** - Accurate inventory prevents overselling
4. **Use categories** - Organization helps you and your customers
5. **Add images** - Products with images sell better
