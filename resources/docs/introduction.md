# Introduction to Folio

Welcome to **Folio** - your all-in-one intelligent business operating system designed for clarity, efficiency, and growth.

## What is Folio?

Folio is a comprehensive inventory management platform that helps small businesses, freelancers, and retailers manage their:

- **Products & Inventory** - Track stock levels, categories, and product details
- **Contacts** - Manage customers, suppliers, and leads in one place
- **Orders** - Create and fulfill orders with real-time inventory updates
- **Invoices** - Generate professional PDF invoices and track payments

## Key Features

### Inventory Management
Keep track of your products with detailed information including SKU, pricing, stock levels, and multiple images. Set up categories to organize your catalog efficiently.

### Contact Management
Build and maintain relationships with your customers and suppliers. Track interactions, set engagement scores, and never lose touch with important contacts.

### Order Processing
Create orders quickly with our multi-step wizard. Automatically update stock levels and generate invoices when orders are fulfilled.

### Professional Invoicing
Generate beautiful PDF invoices with your company branding. Track payment status and send reminders for overdue invoices.

### AI Assistant (Scribe)
Use natural language to query your data, create products, and get insights about your business performance.
